TACTICAL ARCHIVE

Turn Structure: Monster takes its action at the end of each round. Skips turn 1, rolls 1 die on turn 2, then adds 1 die every even turn (4, 6, 8...).

Counters: Monster spells cannot be countered via spells. Mechanical counterspells force an immediate re-roll of that action.

Immunity: Commander damage parameters and literal "You win" deck mechanisms have no effect against the encounter.

Gauntlet Protocols

Boons: Salvaged upon defeating a Lieutenant. Boons act like permanent emblems and stick around for your entire playthrough. Active modules can be exhausted once per round.

Blights: Inflicted via specific dice entries. Blighted cards have negative static traits while in play and require an injection of 3 colorless mana to purge.

Blight Manifest Database:

Rona's Blight (Uncommon)
Put into play under your control when drawn. At the beginning of your upkeep, lose 1 life for each blight token you control.
Purge Cost: Pay {3} to immediately remove the blight and cast the card for free.
