Raid Rules Summary

Turn Structure: Monster goes last. Skips turn 1, rolls 1 die on turn 2, then adds 1 die every even turn.

Counters: Monster spells cannot be countered. Counterspells force a re-roll.

Immunity: Commander damage and "You win" effects do nothing.

Gauntlet Mode Extras

Boons: You draw and choose 1 Boon after defeating a Lieutenant. Boons act like emblems for the rest of the Gauntlet run.

Blights: When a blight effect is rolled, slip a Blight Token into the sleeve of the top card of your deck. When drawn, resolve the Blight immediately. Pay {3} to cleanse it and cast the card for free. A card remains blighted across zones and between bosses until cleansed.

Blight Token Reference:

Rona, Sheoldred’s Faithful (Uncommon Artifact Blight)
Immediately upon being drawn or put into your hand, put this token into play under your control. At the beginning of your upkeep, lose 1 life for each blight token in play.
Activated ability: Pay {3}, Cleanse the blighted card. (You may immediately remove the blight and play the card without paying its mana cost).
"The faithful servants of Sheoldred knew the price of their devotion..."
Ertai Resurrected (Uncommon Artifact Blight)
Immediately upon being drawn or put into your hand, put this token into play under your control. At the beginning of your upkeep sacrifice a creature. If you don't, you lose 3 life.
Activated ability: Pay {3}, Cleanse the blighted card. (You may immediately play the card without paying its mana cost).
"Ertai's return was marked with dark omens..."
Sheoldred, the Apocalypse (Rare Artifact Blight)
Immediately upon being drawn or put into your hand, put this token into play under your control. At the beginning of your upkeep draw two cards and lose two life.
Activated ability: Pay {3}, Cleanse the blighted card. (You may immediately play the card without paying its mana cost).
"The darkness spreads as Sheoldred emerges..."

Choose Your Boon

Select one Boon to keep. The others will be shuffled back into the deck.
