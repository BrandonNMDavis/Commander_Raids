Grimoire Archive

Turn Cycle: The Encounter unleashes its phases at the conclusion of each game round. It remains dormant on Turn 1, casts 1 spell on Turn 2, and dynamically increases output by adding 1 additional die every even round (4, 6, 8...).

Counter-magic: Spells cast from a player deck cannot counter encounter actions. Forcing a counter mechanic via card text commands an immediate re-roll of that action matrix.

Immunities: Commander combat damage specifications and text alterations that declare "You win the game" have no bearing against the entity.

Gauntlet Protocols

Boons: Gathered upon the termination of a Lieutenant. Boons materialize as persistent emblems that endure for the entirety of the campaign run. Triggered modules can be exhausted once per combat match.

Blights: Imposed via catastrophic dice occurrences. Blighted cards manifest heavy operational curses while active in any zone and require a tribute of 3 colorless mana to cleanse.
